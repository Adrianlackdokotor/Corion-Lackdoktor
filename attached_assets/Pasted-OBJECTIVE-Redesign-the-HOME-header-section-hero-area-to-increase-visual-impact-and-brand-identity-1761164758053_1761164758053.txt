OBJECTIVE:
Redesign the HOME header section (hero area) to increase visual impact and brand identity, while maintaining Corion's dark, elegant theme.

TASKS:
1. Replace the current black-and-white background with a more dynamic visual:
   - Use a **colorized workshop image** or **animated background (loop video)** with reflections on car paint, spray gun effect, or detailing scenes.
   - Apply a **dark gradient overlay** (rgba(0,0,0,0.6)) to keep text readable while preserving realism.

2. Enhance the hero text typography:
   - Use **Poppins ExtraBold** or **Oswald** for main titles.
   - Highlight keywords in brand red (#C00020).
   - Example:
     “Präzision trifft Leidenschaft – Ihr Lackdoktor seit 12 Jahren.”

3. Add subtle animations with **Framer Motion**:
   - Fade-in or slide-up for title and call-to-action buttons.
   - Light shimmer effect or slow motion gradient behind text.

4. Introduce a **dual-CTA layout** for conversions:
   - Left button (red): “📸 Foto schicken für Angebot”
   - Right button (outlined): “Jetzt Anrufen”
   - Both buttons should have hover animations and shadows.

5. Keep **Dark Mode** consistent:
   - Background pure black with semi-transparent overlay.
   - Text and buttons in contrast white and brand red.

6. Add a small floating **Chat + WhatsApp zone** fixed at bottom-right, but ensure it does not overlap with the hero text.

7. Optional (recommended for UX):
   - Add a **subtle smoke, light beam, or reflection animation** behind the car shape (using CSS or Lottie animation).
   - Include a background video loop (max 12s, muted, autoplay, no controls) in `public/videos/hero.mp4`.

8. Performance optimization:
   - Lazy load hero video/image.
   - Compress media under 2MB.
   - Maintain mobile responsiveness and proper contrast.

GOAL:
The redesigned homepage header should immediately convey Corion’s professionalism and precision in car painting, creating a visually strong impression with high contrast, motion, and emotional connection.
